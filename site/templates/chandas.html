Chandas is the measure of a verse: how many syllables each pāda has, and which are short (laghu, ।) and which long (guru, ऽ). The metre of every verse was found by the app's own scansion, so it is a reading aid and not an editor's ruling. Choose a metre to see how it is built, how many verses use it in each part of each text, and verses to read.

What “not identified” means

A verse is named only when its syllable counts and patterns fit a metre exactly. Many of the rest are śloka-like verses with one pāda a syllable too long or short (a variant reading, or a sandhi or spelling difference in the digital text), verses of mixed length, or short lines of prose. They are counted here and left unlabelled instead of being guessed.
